## Abrupt-market-move design (proposal only)

1. Keep news-source collection and market-price detection separate. Read only from a future explicitly approved, licensed/authorized time-series provider; do not add MIC dependency to the news shadow now.
2. Require provider observation timestamp, ingestion timestamp, venue/session, currency/unit, and source URL. Reject null/date-only/stale/future observations. The present production market-metrics data fails the intraday requirement.
3. Build a deterministic local shadow-only trigger from rolling returns/volatility on a sufficiently sampled series, session-aware and with explicit data-quality gates. Calibrate on historical data before choosing thresholds; do not invent a percentage threshold from this sample.
4. Emit a trigger record only as a candidate signal, dedupe by instrument/window, and pair it with the existing paid news search. Missing/stale data yields `price_trigger_unavailable` and continues the existing paid fallback unchanged.
5. Minimum instruments for a future separately scoped evaluation: Nikkei futures or index, USD/JPY, WTI/Brent, and S&P/Nasdaq futures. Confirm provider rights and market-session timestamp semantics first.
